# The 2020 DeFi Summer and Yield Farming Boom

## 1. Executive Summary
"DeFi Summer" (mid-2020) was a pivotal technological and financial catalyst that transformed Ethereum from a speculative platform into a functional, decentralized financial ecosystem. The introduction of "liquidity mining" or "yield farming" incentivized users to provide capital to Automated Market Makers (AMMs) and lending protocols in exchange for governance tokens, initiating a massive liquidity black hole that ignited the broader 2020-2021 bull market.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **June 15, 2020:** Compound Finance launches the distribution of its COMP governance token to users borrowing and lending on the protocol. The concept of yield farming is born.
*   **July 2020:** Yearn Finance launches the YFI token with no pre-mine or VC allocation, sparking the "fair launch" meta. YFI surges from $0 to over $40,000 in weeks.
*   **August 2020:** Sushiswap initiates a "vampire attack" on Uniswap by offering SUSHI token incentives to steal liquidity. 
*   **September 16, 2020:** Uniswap responds by launching its UNI token, airdropping 400 UNI (worth ~$1,200 at launch) to all historical users, cementing DeFi's explosive growth.

## 3. Price Impact and Performance Metrics
*   **TVL Explosion:** Total Value Locked (TVL) in DeFi protocols skyrocketed from ~$1 billion in early June to over **$15 billion** by the end of 2020—a 1,400% expansion in underlying liquidity.
*   **Ethereum Price Impact:** ETH, serving as the base layer asset and gas for all transactions, surged from ~$230 in mid-June to ~$480 by September, a **+108%** mid-year rally.
*   **Token Multiples:** Governance tokens like LEND (Aave) and SNX (Synthetix) experienced returns exceeding **+1,000%** within a 90-day window as mercenary capital chased yields.

## 4. Duration of Trend
*   **Days of Core Trend:** The acute "DeFi Summer" mania lasted roughly **90 days** (June to September 2020) before experiencing a sharp multi-week correction in token valuations.
*   **Macro Recovery/Continuation:** Rather than dying, the capital locked in DeFi formed the foundation for the macro cycle. It took roughly **120 days** of consolidation before ETH breached its previous all-time highs in January 2021.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic modeling, DeFi Summer represents a paradigm of **mercenary capital allocation and recursive leverage**.
*   **Hyper-Incentivization:** Market psychology was driven by extreme yield-chasing (greed), with APYs frequently exceeding 10,000% via inflationary token emissions. 
*   **Recursive Borrowing:** Smart money algorithms utilized recursive loops (e.g., depositing ETH to borrow USDC, swapping for more ETH to deposit again) to maximize capital efficiency and farm token rewards.
*   **Liquidity Black Hole:** The structural mechanism permanently altered ETH's supply dynamics. By locking billions of dollars of ETH in smart contracts, float was drastically reduced, creating a massive supply-side shock that violently amplified the upside price action of the base layer asset.
