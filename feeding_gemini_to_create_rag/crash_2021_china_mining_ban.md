# The 2021 China Mining Ban

## 1. Executive Summary
In mid-2021, the Chinese government executed a decisive crackdown on cryptocurrency mining and trading. Given that China hosted an estimated 65% of the global Bitcoin network hashrate at the time, this regulatory exogenous shock triggered extreme network instability, massive miner capitulation, and a severe mid-cycle market crash.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **April 14, 2021:** Bitcoin reaches a high of ~$64,800, fueled by the Coinbase IPO and institutional adoption narratives.
*   **May 12, 2021:** Elon Musk tweets that Tesla will no longer accept Bitcoin due to environmental concerns, initiating market weakness.
*   **May 19, 2021:** Three major Chinese financial industry associations ban financial institutions and payment companies from providing crypto-related services. Market dumps heavily.
*   **May 21, 2021:** The Chinese State Council's Financial Stability and Development Committee officially announces a crackdown on Bitcoin mining and trading activities. 
*   **June 2021:** Chinese provinces (Sichuan, Xinjiang, Inner Mongolia) systematically shut down mining facilities. The Bitcoin network hashrate plummets by over 50%.
*   **July 20, 2021:** Bitcoin bottoms at approximately $29,300.

## 3. Price Impact and Drawdown Metrics
*   **Immediate Crash:** On May 19 alone, BTC fell from ~$43,000 to ~$30,000 intraday, a violent **-30%** daily candle. 
*   **Peak-to-Trough Drawdown:** From the April peak of ~$64,800 to the July low of ~$29,300, Bitcoin suffered a **-54.7%** drawdown. 
*   **Hashrate Impact:** The global hashrate dropped from ~180 EH/s to under 90 EH/s in weeks, forcing a historic negative difficulty adjustment of -27.9% in July.

## 4. Recovery Duration
The network and market demonstrated remarkable resilience as mining equipment relocated to North America and Kazakhstan (The Great Mining Migration).
*   **Days to Recovery:** It took roughly **190 days** from the April peak to fully reclaim and surpass the all-time high. Bitcoin broke $66,000 on **October 20, 2021**.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic modeling, this event highlights **geopolitical risk and miner-induced supply shocks**.
*   **FUD and ESG Narratives:** Market psychology was dominated by Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt (FUD) regarding Bitcoin's environmental impact (ESG) and the potential for a 51% attack due to plummeting hashrate.
*   **Miner Capitulation:** Forced closures meant Chinese miners had to liquidate massive BTC treasuries to cover logistics and relocation costs, flooding the spot market with unexpected supply.
*   **Network Resilience:** Algorithmic signals tracking on-chain data (like Hash Ribbons) generated strong buy signals in late July as the difficulty adjustment finalized, proving the protocol's self-healing game theory.
