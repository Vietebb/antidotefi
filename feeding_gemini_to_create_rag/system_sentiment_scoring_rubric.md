# System Directive: Sentiment Scoring Rubric & Narrative Reversals

## 1. Executive Summary
Algorithmic Natural Language Processing (NLP) must standardize unstructured news into actionable quantitative metrics. This requires a rigid Sentiment Scoring Rubric ranging from -100 (Maximum Bearish / Existential Threat) to +100 (Maximum Bullish / Paradigm Shift). The necessity of this rubric—and the system's ability to rapidly invert its score—is best demonstrated by the January 2024 SEC Twitter Hack, where a +100 signal violently reversed to a -100 signal within minutes.

## 2. Event Timeline (The SEC X Account Hack)
*   **January 9, 2024 (4:11 PM EST):** The official U.S. SEC account on X (a Tier-1 source) tweets that it has officially granted approval for Spot Bitcoin ETFs. (System Score triggers: +100).
*   **4:11 PM - 4:25 PM EST:** Algorithms aggressively buy the headline, assuming ultimate regulatory clearance.
*   **4:26 PM EST:** SEC Chairman Gary Gensler tweets from his personal verified account that the main SEC account was compromised and that "The SEC has NOT approved the listing and trading of spot bitcoin exchange-traded products." (System Score instantly inverts: -100).
*   **4:45 PM EST:** The SEC confirms the hack. X safety teams lock the account.

## 3. Price Impact and Performance Metrics
*   **The Bullish Impulse:** The fake official approval spiked Bitcoin from ~$46,700 to $47,900, a swift **+2.5%** move into heavy pre-existing resistance.
*   **The Bearish Reversal:** The Gensler denial triggered an immediate panic liquidation cascade. BTC plummeted from $47,900 down to a low of ~$45,200, representing a rapid **-5.6%** intraday drawdown.
*   **Volatility Metric:** The localized event caused a temporary 50% spike in short-term implied volatility on options markets as market makers scrambled to re-hedge.

## 4. Duration of Trend and Recovery
*   **Intraday Chaos:** The false-positive event and resulting dump resolved within a **2-hour** window.
*   **Days to Recover:** Because the *actual* Spot ETF approval occurred the very next day (Jan 10, 2024), the market took roughly **24 hours** to recover from the hack-induced volatility and resume its macro uptrend.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & System Directives
The AI must strictly evaluate text against this standardized rubric:
*   **+100 (Maximum Bullish):** Definitive approval of major structural products (ETFs), Tier-1 sovereign adoption (e.g., El Salvador legal tender), Fed emergency rate cuts. *Market Psychology: Euphoria, institutional FOMO.*
*   **+50 (Bullish):** Positive macro data (cooler CPI), major corporate treasury purchases (e.g., MicroStrategy buys).
*   **0 (Neutral):** Expected macro prints matching consensus, standard protocol upgrades.
*   **-50 (Bearish):** Regulatory Wells notices, minor exchange hacks, hot CPI prints.
*   **-100 (Maximum Bearish):** Definitive Tier-1 bans (e.g., China mining ban), major exchange insolvency (e.g., FTX bankruptcy), US government criminal indictments of major founders. *Market Psychology: Panic, existential flight to fiat.*
*   **The Inversion Rule:** If a +100 signal is directly contradicted by an equal or higher Tier-1 authority (e.g., Gensler overriding the hacked SEC account), the system must instantly apply a -100 "Deception Penalty" and market-sell long exposures, as the psychology shifts instantly from greed to extreme risk-aversion.
