# Federal Reserve Interest Rate Hikes and Cuts

## 1. Executive Summary
The US Federal Reserve's monetary policy—specifically the manipulation of the Federal Funds Rate—serves as the primary macroeconomic anchor for cryptocurrency valuations. Because Bitcoin and digital assets yield no native cash flows (traditionally), they are highly sensitive to the risk-free rate of return. The transition from zero-interest-rate policy (ZIRP) in 2020 to aggressive quantitative tightening (QT) in 2022 demonstrated exactly how macro liquidity cycles dictate crypto bull and bear markets.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **March 15, 2020 (Cuts):** The Fed cuts rates to 0.00%-0.25% in response to COVID-19, initiating historic quantitative easing (QE).
*   **November 2021:** The Fed announces the eventual tapering of QE. Crypto markets top.
*   **March 16, 2022 (Hikes):** The Fed executes its first 25 bps rate hike.
*   **June to November 2022:** The Fed executes four consecutive historic 75 bps hikes, aggressively draining global liquidity.
*   **July 2023:** The Fed pauses hikes at a terminal rate of 5.25%-5.50%.

## 3. Price Impact and Performance Metrics
*   **ZIRP Expansion:** Following the March 2020 cuts to zero, Bitcoin embarked on a massive liquidity-driven rally, surging from ~$4,000 to ~$69,000 (a **+1,625%** increase).
*   **Tightening Contraction:** The 2022 rate hike cycle caused catastrophic repricing. From the first taper signals in late 2021 to the depths of the hiking cycle in Nov 2022, BTC experienced a **-77.5%** drawdown.
*   **Altcoin Beta:** Long-duration assets (altcoins) suffered disproportionately, with total altcoin market capitalization dropping **-85%** as the cost of capital soared.

## 4. Duration of Trend and Recovery
*   **Days of Drawdown:** The macro bear market driven by Fed tightening lasted approximately **376 days** (Nov 2021 to Nov 2022).
*   **Days to Recover:** It took exactly **845 days** from the first rate hike in March 2022 for Bitcoin to reclaim its previous all-time highs in March 2024, aided by expectations of a rate cut pivot.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic modeling, Fed policy shifts represent changes in the **discount rate and the price of risk**.
*   **Forward-Discounting:** Crypto markets act as real-time barometers of global liquidity. Algorithms price in expected Fed moves months before they occur (via Fed Funds futures), which is why BTC peaked months before the first actual rate hike.
*   **Risk-On / Risk-Off:** High interest rates provide a lucrative "risk-free" yield in US Treasuries, incentivizing capital flight away from speculative, high-volatility assets like crypto.
*   **Macro Reflexivity:** Tightening financial conditions broke over-leveraged crypto entities (Celsius, 3AC, FTX), transitioning a standard macro drawdown into a systemic contagion event.
