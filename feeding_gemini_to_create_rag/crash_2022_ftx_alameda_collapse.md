# The 2022 FTX and Alameda Collapse

## 1. Executive Summary
The bankruptcy of FTX, one of the world's largest cryptocurrency exchanges, and its sister trading firm, Alameda Research, in November 2022 stands as the most devastating fraud-driven systemic shock in modern crypto history. The event wiped out billions in customer deposits, destroyed market liquidity, and catalyzed intense global regulatory scrutiny, plunging the market to the lowest depths of the 2022 bear market.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **November 2, 2022:** CoinDesk publishes a leaked balance sheet showing Alameda Research is highly illiquid and heavily dependent on FTT, an exchange token printed out of thin air by FTX.
*   **November 6, 2022:** Binance CEO Changpeng Zhao (CZ) announces Binance will liquidate its entire FTT treasury. Panic selling begins.
*   **November 8, 2022:** FTX experiences a devastating bank run, processing $6 billion in withdrawals before completely halting them. FTT collapses by 80%. Binance signs a non-binding LOI to acquire FTX but backs out the next day after corporate due diligence.
*   **November 11, 2022:** Sam Bankman-Fried resigns. FTX, Alameda, and 130 affiliated companies file for Chapter 11 bankruptcy.
*   **November 21, 2022:** Bitcoin hits the macro bear market absolute bottom of ~$15,476.

## 3. Price Impact and Drawdown Metrics
*   **Immediate Crash:** Bitcoin fell from ~$21,300 on November 5 to a low of ~$15,476 on November 21—a rapid **-27.3%** drop.
*   **FTT Wipeout:** The FTX exchange token fell from ~$25 to under $1.50, wiping out **-94%** of its value in less than a week.
*   **Macro Context:** This event marked the terminal capitulation of the 2022 bear market, finalizing a total peak-to-trough drawdown for Bitcoin of **-77.5%** (from the $69k peak in 2021).

## 4. Recovery Duration
The structural deficit caused by FTX (known as the "Alameda Gap") led to historically low liquidity in crypto order books.
*   **Days to Recovery:** Bitcoin took approximately **60 days** to recover from the $15.4k bottom and reclaim the pre-FTX level of $21,300 in mid-January 2023. However, reclaiming true macro momentum took over a year.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic modeling, the FTX collapse is a masterclass in **counterparty risk, fraudulent off-chain accounting, and liquidity vacuums**.
*   **Absolute Mistrust:** Psychology reverted to maximum paranoia. Billions of dollars were pulled from centralized exchanges (CEX) into cold storage, creating a permanent shift in market custody dynamics.
*   **The Alameda Gap:** Alameda Research provided a massive percentage of market-making liquidity globally. Their instantaneous removal hollowed out order books, causing extreme algorithmic slippage and increased volatility for months.
*   **Bank Run Dynamics:** The crash proved that in a transparent, 24/7 liquid market, rumors of insolvency transition to fatal bank runs at algorithmic speed, far faster than traditional finance circuit breakers can handle.
