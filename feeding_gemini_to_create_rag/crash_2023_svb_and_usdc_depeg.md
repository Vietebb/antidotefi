# The 2023 Silicon Valley Bank & USDC Depeg

## 1. Executive Summary
In March 2023, the collapse of Silicon Valley Bank (SVB)—the second-largest bank failure in U.S. history—sent shockwaves through the cryptocurrency ecosystem. The crisis directly impacted Circle, the issuer of USD Coin (USDC), revealing massive fiat reserve exposure. This triggered a historic depeg of one of the crypto market's foundational stablecoins, testing the resilience of decentralized finance (DeFi) primitives.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **March 8, 2023:** SVB announces a $1.8 billion loss on treasury sales and attempts to raise capital. A bank run is initiated by venture capitalists.
*   **March 10, 2023:** California regulators shut down SVB. After market close, Circle announces that $3.3 billion of the $40 billion cash reserves backing USDC are stuck at SVB.
*   **March 11, 2023:** Panic ensues over the weekend. USDC heavily depegs, dropping as low as $0.87 across decentralized exchanges (DEXs). DeFi liquidity pools are aggressively drained.
*   **March 12, 2023:** The U.S. Treasury, Federal Reserve, and FDIC announce a systemic risk exception, guaranteeing all SVB depositors will be made whole. 
*   **March 13, 2023:** Circle successfully regains access to its funds; USDC restores its $1.00 peg.

## 3. Price Impact and Drawdown Metrics
*   **Stablecoin Depeg:** USDC dropped by **-13%** (reaching ~$0.87), causing chaos in DeFi protocols relying on its $1 parity. Secondary stablecoins like DAI also depegged to ~$0.90 due to heavy collateralization in USDC.
*   **Bitcoin Impact:** Bitcoin initially dropped from ~$22,000 to ~$19,500 (**-11.3%**) as the banking panic peaked on March 10.
*   **Safe Haven Rally:** Upon the Fed's liquidity intervention (BTFP), Bitcoin aggressively reversed. From the $19,500 low, BTC rallied to over $28,000 within a week, a **+43%** surge.

## 4. Recovery Duration
Because the shock was contained by government intervention, the recovery was extraordinarily rapid.
*   **Days to Recovery (USDC):** The stablecoin reclaimed its peg in just **3 days**.
*   **Days to Recovery (BTC):** Bitcoin recovered its pre-SVB drop within **48 hours** and immediately triggered a massive bullish continuation.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic models tracking macro-crypto correlations, the SVB crisis marks a definitive **paradigm shift and safe-haven decoupling**.
*   **Fiat Contagion:** The psychology shifted rapidly. The market realized that traditional banking systems posed a counterparty risk to crypto, not the other way around.
*   **DeFi Arbitrage:** The depeg triggered violent arbitrage behavior. Algorithms frantically swapped USDC for USDT (Tether) or aggressively bought BTC using discounted USDC, contributing to Bitcoin's violent upside wick.
*   **The "Digital Gold" Narrative:** As traditional bank stocks plummeted, algorithmic traders and institutional capital flowed heavily into Bitcoin. BTC acted as an anti-fragile asset, fundamentally validating its core narrative as an alternative to fractional reserve banking.
