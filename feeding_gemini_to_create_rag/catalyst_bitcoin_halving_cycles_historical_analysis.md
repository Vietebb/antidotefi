# Bitcoin Halving Cycles: Historical Supply Shock Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary
The Bitcoin Halving is a programmatic event occurring every 210,000 blocks (roughly four years), reducing the block reward issued to miners by 50%. This predictable supply shock serves as the fundamental macroeconomic catalyst for Bitcoin's four-year market cycles. Historical data across the 2012, 2016, 2020, and 2024 epochs demonstrates a consistent pattern of post-halving price discovery driven by an inelastic supply meeting sustained or growing demand.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **Epoch 1 (Nov 28, 2012):** Reward reduced from 50 BTC to 25 BTC.
*   **Epoch 2 (Jul 9, 2016):** Reward reduced from 25 BTC to 12.5 BTC.
*   **Epoch 3 (May 11, 2020):** Reward reduced from 12.5 BTC to 6.25 BTC.
*   **Epoch 4 (Apr 19, 2024):** Reward reduced from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC.

## 3. Price Impact and Performance Metrics
The percentage impact of the halving is historically asymmetric, characterized by massive parabolic bull runs.
*   **2012 Impact:** BTC rallied from ~$12 at the halving to a peak of ~$1,150 in late 2013, an astonishing **+9,480%** increase.
*   **2016 Impact:** BTC rallied from ~$650 to ~$19,783 in late 2017, a **+2,940%** increase.
*   **2020 Impact:** BTC rallied from ~$8,800 to ~$69,000 in late 2021, a **+684%** increase.
*   *Law of Diminishing Returns:* As market capitalization expands, the percentage return of each subsequent cycle predictably compresses due to the sheer volume of capital required to move the market.

## 4. Duration of Trend (Days to Peak)
Unlike sudden market crashes, halvings initiate protracted, multi-month uptrends.
*   **2012 Cycle:** It took **365 days** post-halving to reach the cycle top.
*   **2016 Cycle:** It took **526 days** post-halving to reach the cycle top.
*   **2020 Cycle:** It took **548 days** post-halving to reach the cycle top.
*   This establishes a robust algorithmic baseline indicating cycle peaks typically occur 12 to 18 months following the supply reduction.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic modeling, halvings represent a **deterministic supply contraction and anticipated stock-to-flow repricing**.
*   **Miner Accumulation:** Months prior to the halving, efficient miners accumulate hardware and hold BTC, constricting floating supply.
*   **Self-Fulfilling Prophecy:** The market psychology is heavily reliant on the "four-year cycle" narrative. Algorithmic and discretionary buyers front-run the event, creating a reflexive upward loop.
*   **Post-Halving Supply Deficit:** Post-event, the structural daily sell pressure from miners is cut in half. If daily fiat inflows remain constant, the price must algorithmically clear at a higher equilibrium, triggering momentum trading models.
