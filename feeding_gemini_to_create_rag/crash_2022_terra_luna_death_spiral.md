# The 2022 Terra (LUNA) Death Spiral

## 1. Executive Summary
The collapse of the Terra ecosystem in May 2022 was an endogenous algorithmic failure that erased $60 billion in market value in a matter of days. Terra's algorithmic stablecoin, UST, relied on a seigniorage mechanism tied to a volatile governance token, LUNA. When the peg broke, it triggered a hyperinflationary death spiral, fundamentally altering the macro landscape of the cryptocurrency market and igniting a widespread contagion event.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **May 7, 2022:** A series of large, coordinated UST withdrawals from Anchor Protocol and Curve Finance causes UST to slightly depeg to $0.98. 
*   **May 9, 2022:** The Luna Foundation Guard (LFG) deploys $1.5 billion in Bitcoin reserves to defend the peg, failing to stem the tide. UST drops to $0.60. Panic ensues.
*   **May 11-12, 2022:** The algorithmic mint/burn mechanism hyperinflates the LUNA supply from 340 million to 6.5 trillion in a frantic, failed attempt to absorb UST sell pressure. 
*   **May 13, 2022:** The Terra blockchain is officially halted. LUNA trades at fractions of a cent ($0.00001), and UST is effectively worthless.

## 3. Price Impact and Drawdown Metrics
*   **Asset Annihilation:** LUNA collapsed from an all-time high of ~$119 in April to $0 within 30 days—a **-99.99%** wipeout.
*   **Bitcoin Impact:** LFG's forced liquidation of ~80,000 BTC crashed Bitcoin from ~$39,000 to ~$26,000 within a week, a **-33%** drop.
*   **Contagion Drawdown:** This event initiated a broader market crash, ultimately bringing Bitcoin down to ~$17,500 by mid-June, representing a total macro drawdown of **-74%** from the 2021 peak.

## 4. Recovery Duration
The Terra collapse caused severe structural damage, resulting in the failure of over-leveraged entities like Three Arrows Capital (3AC) and Celsius Network. 
*   **Days to Recovery:** The market entered a deep crypto winter. It took precisely **573 days** for Bitcoin to reclaim the pre-crash level of $39,000, finally breaching it in **December 2023**.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic risk management, Terra represents the apex of **flawed tokenomics and endogenous reflexivity**.
*   **Loss of Confidence:** The market shifted from blind trust in yield-bearing assets (Anchor's 20% APY) to profound skepticism of all algorithmic peg mechanisms.
*   **Hyperinflationary Mathematics:** Trading algorithms failed to price in the unbounded minting function of LUNA. Once the market cap of LUNA fell below the market cap of UST, the system became mathematically insolvent.
*   **Systemic Contagion:** The psychology shifted to counterparty paranoia. Market participants realized massive institutional funds were exposed to the same localized risk, transforming a single project failure into a macro-credit crisis.
