# Exchange Inflows and Outflows: Supply Dynamics

## 1. Executive Summary
Tracking the movement of Bitcoin and crypto assets into and out of centralized exchange wallets provides critical on-chain foresight into imminent market volatility. "Inflows" typically signal an intent to sell or use assets as derivative collateral, increasing supply overhang. Conversely, "Outflows" indicate a transition to cold storage, signaling long-term accumulation and a reduction in floating supply. The FTX collapse in 2022 triggered the most violent structural shift in exchange balance metrics in crypto history.

## 2. Event Timeline (The 2022 FTX Bank Run Application)
*   **November 2, 2022:** The balance sheet of Alameda Research is leaked, sparking initial institutional concern. Minor exchange outflows begin across the ecosystem.
*   **November 6, 2022:** Binance announces the liquidation of its FTT tokens. Intense panic initiates a historic bank run on FTX.
*   **November 7-9, 2022:** Over 80,000 BTC is withdrawn from exchanges globally within 48 hours as market participants realize counterparty risks. FTX halts withdrawals.
*   **November 11, 2022:** FTX files for bankruptcy. In the subsequent 30 days, a record-breaking 200,000 BTC is withdrawn from global centralized exchanges into self-custody.

## 3. Price Impact and Performance Metrics
*   **Initial Price Shock:** The sudden realization of insolvency and the liquidation of FTX collateral drove Bitcoin from ~$21,300 to ~$15,476, a **-27.3%** drop.
*   **Supply Squeeze:** Despite the price crash, the macro metric shifted bullishly. Total exchange balances dropped to a 4-year low of roughly 2.2 million BTC (representing only ~11% of circulating supply).
*   **Illiquidity Premium:** As coins moved off-exchange, market depth thinned dramatically, causing a 2x increase in algorithmic slippage on major pairs for the remainder of Q4 2022.

## 4. Duration of Trend and Recovery
*   **Outflow Duration:** The extreme panic-driven outflow trend persisted for roughly **45 days** before exchange balances stabilized at their new structural lows.
*   **Price Recovery:** It took Bitcoin approximately **60 days** from the $15.4k bottom to recover back to pre-FTX levels ($21,300), driven by the reality that the artificial paper-supply created by FTX had been eliminated, making the remaining spot supply highly inelastic.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic modeling, exchange flows map the transition between **speculative inventory and sovereign custody**.
*   **Intent to Sell:** High velocity inflows (e.g., >10,000 BTC entering Coinbase in an hour) historically act as leading indicators for localized market tops, triggering quantitative models to front-run the anticipated spot selling.
*   **Not Your Keys, Not Your Coins:** The FTX event fundamentally reprogrammed investor psychology. Extreme fear of exchange insolvency paradoxically created the most bullish supply dynamic possible: forced diamond-handing.
*   **The Inelastic Squeeze:** Algorithms track exchange balances because when supply is deeply depleted by massive outflows, it requires significantly less fiat capital to drive the price upward, paving the way for violent upside volatility.
