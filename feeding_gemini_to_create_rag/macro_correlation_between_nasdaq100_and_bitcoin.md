# Correlation Between Nasdaq 100 (NDX) and Bitcoin

## 1. Executive Summary
Historically touted as an uncorrelated safe-haven asset, Bitcoin's macroeconomic behavior shifted fundamentally post-2020. As institutional capital entered the digital asset space, Bitcoin became highly financialized, increasingly trading as a high-beta technology proxy. The correlation between Bitcoin and the Nasdaq 100 (NDX) reached unprecedented positive levels during the 2022 tightening cycle, profoundly impacting algorithmic pairs trading and risk-parity models.

## 2. Event Timeline
*   **2010 - 2019 (Uncorrelated Era):** Bitcoin exhibits a near-zero to slightly negative rolling correlation with traditional equities. 
*   **March 2020 (The Covid Convergence):** Both NDX and BTC collapse simultaneously during the global dash for cash. Correlation begins to persistently trend positive.
*   **May 2022:** The 90-day rolling Pearson correlation coefficient between Bitcoin and the Nasdaq 100 hits an all-time high of **0.82**.
*   **March 2023 (The SVB Decoupling):** During the regional banking crisis, NDX faces pressure while BTC rallies as a decentralized flight-to-safety, breaking the correlation heavily back toward 0 for the first time in years.

## 3. Price Impact and Performance Metrics
*   **Beta Amplification:** During the 2022 bear market, Bitcoin acted as a levered version of the Nasdaq. When the NDX dropped **-35%** peak-to-trough, BTC dropped **-77%**, exhibiting a beta of roughly 2.2 relative to tech stocks.
*   **Decoupling Divergence:** In mid-March 2023, NDX traded flat to slightly down over a two-week period, while Bitcoin rallied **+40%**, aggressively breaking the algorithmic correlation models.

## 4. Duration of Trend and Recovery
*   **Duration of High Correlation:** The period of strict, high positive correlation (>0.6) lasted for roughly **18 months** (late 2021 to early 2023), dominated entirely by central bank liquidity cycles.
*   **Decoupling Cycle:** It took **14 days** during the March 2023 banking crisis to completely reset the 90-day correlation matrix, forcing a massive reallocation among quantitative funds.

## 5. Core Market Psychology & Microstructure
For algorithmic modeling, this dynamic highlights the shift from **retail insurgency to institutional risk-parity allocation**.
*   **Institutional Categorization:** Wall Street algorithms categorize Bitcoin structurally as a long-duration, high-volatility technology asset. If risk-off parameters trigger selling in unprofitable software stocks, they automatically trigger selling in Bitcoin.
*   **Algorithmic Arbitrage:** High-frequency statistical arbitrage models explicitly trade the spread between NDX futures and BTC spot. When BTC lags an NDX rally, algos systematically buy BTC to close the stat-arb gap.
*   **The Safe Haven Narrative:** The 2023 decoupling proved that while BTC trades like tech during peacetime, extreme banking stress re-activates its original psychological narrative as "digital gold," overriding the equity correlation algorithms.
